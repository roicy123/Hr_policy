NOTICE PERIOD AND SEPARATION POLICY
Policy No: HR-POL-06 | Effective: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains the notice requirements, exit process, and final settlement when an employee leaves the Company. It applies to all full-time employees, whether they resign or the Company ends their employment.

2. NOTICE PERIOD
2.1 During probation (first 6 months): 15 days' notice by either side.
2.2 After confirmation, for Grades L1 to L3: 60 days' notice by either side.
2.3 For Grades L4 and above, including managers and team leads: 90 days' notice by either side.
2.4 Notice periods are counted in calendar days, including weekends and holidays, starting from the day the resignation is accepted.

3. HOW TO RESIGN
3.1 Submit a resignation email to your reporting manager and HR, and file Form HR-12 on the HR portal.
3.2 The manager acknowledges the resignation within 2 working days. HR confirms the last working day in writing within 5 working days.
3.3 A resignation can be withdrawn within 7 days of submission with manager and HR approval. After that, withdrawal is at the Company's discretion.

4. LEAVE DURING NOTICE PERIOD
4.1 Employees cannot take planned casual leave during the notice period without Department Head approval. Sick leave with a medical certificate is allowed.
4.2 Unplanned absence during notice, beyond 3 days, extends the notice period by the same number of days.
4.3 Leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period, except as described in section 5.

5. NOTICE BUY-OUT AND EARLY RELEASE
5.1 Employees may request early release by paying salary in lieu of the remaining notice period, calculated on gross monthly salary divided by 30 per day.
5.2 Early release is subject to Department Head approval and depends on project handover. The Company may refuse a buy-out when critical client work is in progress.
5.3 The Company may waive part or all of the notice period at its discretion. If the Company waives notice, no recovery is made from the employee.
5.4 Unused earned leave may be adjusted against a shortfall in notice, up to a maximum of 10 days, with HR approval.

6. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER AND HANDOVER
6.1 Within the first 5 working days of notice, the employee and manager prepare a handover plan covering projects, documentation, credentials, and client contacts.
6.2 All code, documents, and access must be handed over by the last working day. Failure to complete handover can delay the final settlement.
6.3 The employee should train their replacement or a nominated colleague for at least 10 working days where possible.

7. EXIT PROCESS
7.1 Exit interview with HR is held in the last week of employment.
7.2 Return all Company assets, including laptop, access card, and any equipment, by the last working day.
7.3 IT disables system access at 6:00 PM on the last working day. The employee must not retain Company data on personal devices.
7.4 A no-dues clearance form must be signed by IT, Finance, Admin, and the reporting manager.

8. FINAL SETTLEMENT
8.1 The full and final settlement includes pending salary, encashment of earned leave, and any gratuity or bonus owed.
8.2 Settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day, once clearance is complete.
8.3 Recoveries include any unsettled advances, notice shortfall, unreturned assets, and training cost repayments due under the expense policy.
8.4 Earned leave can be encashed up to a maximum of 30 days. Casual and sick leave cannot be encashed.

9. DOCUMENTS ISSUED
9.1 A relieving letter and experience letter are issued within 10 working days of the final settlement.
9.2 Employment verification requests from future employers are answered by HR within 3 working days and confirm only dates of service and designation.

10. TERMINATION BY THE COMPANY
10.1 For performance reasons, the employee is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan before termination is considered.
10.2 For serious misconduct, the Company may terminate employment immediately following a show-cause process, without notice pay or relieving benefits other than statutory dues.
10.3 For redundancy or restructuring, the Company provides notice as in section 2, or severance pay of 1 month of gross salary for each year of service, with a minimum of 2 months.

11. RE-HIRING
Former employees who left in good standing may be re-hired after a cooling-off period of 6 months. Employees terminated for misconduct are not eligible for re-hire.
