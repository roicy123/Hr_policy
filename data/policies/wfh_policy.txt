WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Policy No: HR-POL-02 | Effective: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains when and how employees may work remotely. It applies to all full-time employees who have completed their probation period. The Company follows a hybrid model: most teams work from the office on collaboration days and from home on focus days.

2. ELIGIBILITY
2.1 Full-time confirmed employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
2.2 Employees on probation (first 6 months) are not eligible for regular work from home. A manager may approve up to 2 ad-hoc WFH days per month in genuine cases.
2.3 Interns and trainees must work from the office on all working days.
2.4 Employees with 3 or more years of service and a "Meets Expectations" or higher rating may apply for an extended hybrid arrangement of up to 3 WFH days per week. Extended arrangements need approval from the Department Head.

3. REQUESTING WORK FROM HOME
3.1 Submit a request on the HR portal using Form HR-05 at least 1 working day in advance.
3.2 Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 1 working day. Requests with no response after 1 working day are escalated to the Department Head.
3.3 Recurring weekly WFH days may be agreed once for a period of up to 3 months and renewed after that.
3.4 Fixed office days apply to everyone: every Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory in-office days for all teams, and WFH is not permitted on these days except with Department Head approval.

4. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
4.1 Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. Employees must be online and reachable on the company chat tool during core hours.
4.2 Standard working hours remain 9 hours per day, including a 1-hour break.
4.3 Attendance on WFH days is marked through the HR portal by 10:30 AM. Failure to mark attendance for 3 days in a month may be treated as unauthorised absence.
4.4 Employees must attend all scheduled meetings with their camera on unless the meeting organiser says otherwise.

5. EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCES
5.1 The Company provides one laptop per employee. Laptops must be used only for work and kept updated with the latest security patches.
5.2 A one-time home-office setup allowance of Rs. 8,000 is available to confirmed employees who work from home at least 2 days a week. Claim it with bills within 60 days of approval using Form FIN-07.
5.3 A monthly internet reimbursement of up to Rs. 1,000 is paid against a valid broadband bill for employees with an approved hybrid arrangement.
5.4 Personal devices must not be used to store confidential Company data.

6. INFORMATION SECURITY WHILE REMOTE
6.1 Connect to Company systems only through the approved VPN.
6.2 Do not use public Wi-Fi for work without VPN. Do not work in public places where your screen can be seen by others.
6.3 Lock your screen whenever you step away. Sensitive documents must not be printed at home.
6.4 Report any lost or stolen device to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours of discovering the loss.

7. PERFORMANCE AND REVIEW
7.1 Performance is measured by deliverables, not by hours online. Managers review WFH arrangements every 3 months.
7.2 The Company may withdraw WFH privileges with 5 working days' notice if performance drops, security rules are broken, or business needs change.
7.3 Employees working from a different city or state without approval will be treated as in breach of this policy, since tax and legal compliance differs by location.

8. EXCEPTIONS
8.1 Medical conditions, pregnancy, and caregiving emergencies may qualify for temporary full-remote work of up to 4 weeks. Submit supporting documents to HR.
8.2 During natural disasters, severe weather, or public emergencies, the Company may declare mandatory work from home for all staff by email.

9. CONTACT
Questions about this policy can be sent to hr-helpdesk@company.example.
