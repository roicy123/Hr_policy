EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY
Policy No: HR-POL-05 | Effective: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE AND ELIGIBILITY
This policy describes the benefits available to employees. Unless stated otherwise, benefits apply to full-time employees from their date of joining. Interns and contractors are not eligible except where mentioned.

2. HEALTH INSURANCE
2.1 Every full-time employee is covered under a group health insurance policy with a sum insured of Rs. 5,00,000 per year, effective from the first day of employment.
2.2 Coverage extends to the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children. Parents can be added during open enrolment by paying an additional premium of Rs. 9,000 per year per parent through payroll deduction.
2.3 The policy covers hospitalisation, pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses for 30 and 60 days respectively, day-care procedures, and ambulance charges up to Rs. 3,000.
2.4 Pre-existing conditions are covered after a waiting period of 12 months.
2.5 Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals. For non-network hospitals, submit a reimbursement claim within 30 days of discharge.
2.6 Open enrolment is held every year in March. New joiners must add dependants within 30 days of joining.

3. TERM LIFE AND ACCIDENT COVER
The Company provides term life insurance of 3 times annual fixed salary and personal accident cover of Rs. 10,00,000 at no cost to the employee.

4. PROVIDENT FUND AND GRATUITY
4.1 Both the employee and the Company contribute 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF).
4.2 Gratuity is payable after 5 years of continuous service, calculated as 15 days of last drawn basic salary for each completed year of service, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.

5. MATERNITY, PATERNITY, AND PARENTAL BENEFITS
5.1 Maternity leave is 26 weeks for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onward, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
5.2 Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
5.3 Adoption leave of 12 weeks is available to adoptive parents of a child under 3 months.
5.4 The Company runs a tie-up with a creche near the Kochi office, with a 50% fee subsidy up to Rs. 5,000 per month for children up to age 5.

6. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
6.1 Each employee has an annual learning budget of Rs. 40,000 for certifications and courses, subject to manager approval. Claims follow the expense policy.
6.2 Employees get 2 paid learning days per year to attend conferences or complete exams.
6.3 Internal workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions are held on the second Friday of every month.

7. WELLNESS
7.1 An annual health check-up worth up to Rs. 4,000 is provided for every employee aged 30 and above, and a basic check-up worth Rs. 2,000 for employees below 30.
7.2 Free, confidential counselling is available through the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), up to 6 sessions per year per employee, available 24x7 on 1800-111-1111.
7.3 A gym or sports reimbursement of up to Rs. 6,000 per year is available against bills.

8. FOOD AND TRANSPORT
8.1 Subsidised meals are served at the office cafeteria, with the employee paying Rs. 40 per meal.
8.2 Shuttle service operates on fixed routes for employees working in the Kochi office. Employees working late after 9:00 PM are provided cab drop with a guard for female employees.

9. FESTIVE AND RECOGNITION BENEFITS
9.1 A festive bonus of Rs. 5,000 is paid to all confirmed employees in the month of Onam or Diwali, as chosen by the employee at the start of the year.
9.2 Service awards are given at 3, 5, and 10 years of service, with a cash value of Rs. 5,000, Rs. 15,000, and Rs. 50,000 respectively.
9.3 The Referral Bonus is Rs. 25,000 for each successful hire, paid after the referred employee completes 6 months. This applies to all roles except HR and senior leadership.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
The Company may revise these benefits with 30 days' notice. Statutory benefits will always follow current law.
