LEAVE POLICY
Policy No: HR-POL-01 | Effective: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

1. Casual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year.
A maximum of 3 casual leaves may be taken consecutively.
Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 day in advance through the HR portal.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 8 sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Carry Forward
Up to 5 unused casual leaves may be carried forward to the next year.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward and lapses on 31 December.

4. Probation
Employees on probation (first 6 months) accrue casual leave at 1 day per month of service.

5. Earned Leave
Confirmed employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave per month of service (18 days per year).
Earned leave can be accumulated up to 45 days and encashed on separation, up to 30 days.

6. Public Holidays
The Company observes 12 paid public holidays per year, published each January.
