EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Policy No: FIN-POL-01 | Effective: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Finance Department

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy defines which business expenses the Company reimburses, the limits that apply, and how to claim them. It applies to all employees. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business, and approved in advance where this policy says so.

2. TRAVEL
2.1 Air travel: Economy class only. Domestic flights must be booked at least 7 days in advance through the Company travel desk. Employees at Grade L5 and above may travel in premium economy on flights longer than 4 hours.
2.2 Train travel: AC 2-tier is allowed for employees up to Grade L3. AC 1st class or Executive Chair Car is allowed for Grade L4 and above.
2.3 Local travel: Use of cab services is reimbursed up to Rs. 1,500 per day for client visits. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at Rs. 8 per km for two-wheelers and Rs. 12 per km for cars, with a trip log.
2.4 Travel by an employee's own preference for a more expensive option is reimbursed only up to the cost of the standard option.

3. ACCOMMODATION
3.1 Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the following limits per night, including taxes:
- Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): Rs. 6,000
- Tier 2 cities (Kochi, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and similar): Rs. 4,000
- Other locations: Rs. 3,000
3.2 Employees must book through the Company travel desk wherever possible. Stays longer than 5 nights need approval from the Department Head.

4. MEALS AND ALLOWANCES
4.1 Daily meal allowance while travelling is Rs. 1,200 in Tier 1 cities and Rs. 900 elsewhere. No bills are needed up to this limit.
4.2 Client entertainment meals are reimbursed up to Rs. 2,500 per person and require prior manager approval and a list of attendees.
4.3 Alcohol is never reimbursable.
4.4 Overtime meals are reimbursed up to Rs. 300 when an employee works beyond 9:00 PM on an approved project deadline.

5. COMMUNICATION AND OTHER EXPENSES
5.1 Mobile phone bills are reimbursed up to Rs. 600 per month for employees in client-facing roles.
5.2 Professional certifications and training: pre-approved courses are reimbursed in full up to Rs. 40,000 per year. The employee must stay with the Company for 12 months after claiming, or repay the amount in full.
5.3 Books and learning subscriptions are reimbursed up to Rs. 5,000 per year.

6. APPROVAL LIMITS
6.1 Claims up to Rs. 10,000 are approved by the reporting manager.
6.2 Claims from Rs. 10,001 to Rs. 50,000 also need Department Head approval.
6.3 Claims above Rs. 50,000 need Finance Controller approval.
6.4 Employees cannot approve their own claims or the claims of their direct manager.

7. HOW TO CLAIM
7.1 Submit Form FIN-07 on the finance portal with scanned original bills for every expense above Rs. 500.
7.2 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days need Department Head approval, and claims older than 90 days are rejected.
7.3 Bills must show the vendor name, date, amount, and GST number where applicable. Handwritten bills and screenshots without payment proof are not accepted.
7.4 Finance reviews claims within 5 working days of approval. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary, or within 10 working days if raised as an urgent claim.

8. ADVANCES
8.1 Employees may request a travel advance of up to 70% of estimated costs using Form FIN-03, at least 5 working days before travel.
8.2 Advances must be settled within 7 days of returning from the trip. Unsettled advances are deducted from the next salary.

9. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Fines and penalties, personal entertainment, spouse or family travel, minibar and laundry beyond 3 nights of stay, gifts to clients above Rs. 2,000, and any expense without a valid bill are not reimbursed.

10. MISUSE
False or inflated claims are treated as serious misconduct and may lead to recovery of the amount, disciplinary action, or termination.
